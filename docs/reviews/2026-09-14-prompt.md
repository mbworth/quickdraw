# Prompt strategy review — 2026-09-14

Four Opus reviewers on the recorded games (runs 44–56) and the game32 prompt: loss forensics, win forensics, a line-by-line critique of the strategy text, and the harness/test contract the prompt sits in. Reviewed and synthesised here; the result is `prompts/ashfall/game38-sonnet.md` plus `--ashfall-guide`.

## What the games say

**Losses (31, 34, 36, 37)** share one shape. Barracks 0:35–1:17, harvesters to 18–23, riflemen to exactly 8 by 2:18–3:09, then the 8 attack-move at the packet's `search` waypoint with no turret up (0 of 4). The waypoint moved (9 hops in 115 s in game 37, 3 in game 36, twice onto crystal pockets, once back into my own half), the group zig-zagged 100–150 units, met the AI's dug-in riflemen or its wave in the open in 2–4 pieces, and died in 5–20 s. The AI's wave of 5–8 arrived at my base on schedule at 3:45–4:30 and found 0–1 defenders; the harvester line died in a 60–90 s block; ore climbed to 350 against a `queue-full` single barracks; the core fell 2–3 minutes later. Trooper train orders sent: 16 / 40 / 22 / 36 against 70 in the win on the same prompt. Games 32 and 33 (fixed wrong destination near the centre) were stalemates, not losses: the base dies only when the army crosses the map.

**Wins (27, 28, 30, 35)** are one object: a group of 11–31 riflemen (median 17) that left home 12–53 s after the last enemy unit died at my base, walked ~130 units, met 0–1 defenders and killed a 1300-hp core in 17–45 s (118 s and nearly failed with 10). My core took 5 hp of damage across the four games. Every 8-rifleman sally at 2:35–3:08 failed and came home. Defence was dug-in riflemen (71 of 84 kills near my core), not the turret. Two barracks, 18–27 harvesters, no crystal, no upgrades. Target acquisition was the fragile link: each win found the core a different way (the old example coordinate, chasing a wave home, a remembered depot, the `f3 enemy` field); the field-first search pointed away 5 of 5 times in game 35 (the win came after it stopped). In every recorded game the enemy core sat within 25 of the mirror of my core.

**Model-reading defects** (secondary, decisive twice): `A` and `X` print riflemen in the same `tr xN@x,z` shape and the model pasted the enemy label 13 calls running while its base burned (game 36); it sign-flipped the waypoint twice (game 33); `am army` with one rifleman (game 31).

## What was wrong with the text

The critique's findings, the ones that changed the rewrite: the purchase list read as a menu, so the cheapest match ("under 8 harvesters") won every opening; "ball", "anchor", "dug in", "map-centre side", "income idle", "production capped" were undefined; "again every time the ball is back to 8", "reinforcements rally 20 short of their base", "join in groups of 4+", "unhurt" all need memory the model does not have; rule 3's "never send the ball back to the anchor while it is unhurt" contradicted rules 2 and 6 and forbade the right answer to the 3:00 wave; the standing-state assertions ("is always on its way to the search point") were satisfied by re-issuing the move every packet; "Its base is empty right after a wave fails" was the most actionable sentence and no rule used it. The build order is affordable (turret + 8 riflemen by ~2:00 with ~90 ore spare); the trigger was wrong, not the budget: 8 riflemen make 49 dps against buildings and arrive one at a time thereafter.

## Decisions

1. **Commit gate is 15 riflemen, turret up, no enemy combat unit visible**, and the text names the packet to do it on (`T kill` empties `X`). Under 15 never leave the post.
2. **The barracks rally point is the attack flag.** Commit = `am troopers <target>; ry <barracks> <target>`. Rally out (more than 30 from home) means the attack is on: idle riflemen anywhere go to the target, new ones walk there on their own; rally at the post means any group out comes back. Stateless, readable from `P`.
3. **No jargon, no coordinates, no arithmetic in the text.** Words defined once (home, post, yard, target, out, rally out). Every position is pasted from `B` or `M`.
4. **Two ladders and a housekeeping list**, first match wins, one purchase and one army order a packet. Barracks before any harvester; turret before riflemen; second barracks at 200 ore with every queue at 2+; never a third.
5. **Packet features under `--ashfall-guide`** so the rules need no counting or geometry: `wk N tr N` on `H`; `post@x,z` (8 toward the centre) and `yard@x,z` (12 away) on the compact `B` line; `(no tu)` on `B` while no turret exists; `out` after a rifleman cluster in `A` or a rally in `P` more than 30 from home; no `hb` on `T`; the search waypoint is the mirror of my core until a combat unit stands within 12 of it, then the nearest unexplored ore field. Off, the packet is byte-identical.
6. **Dropped**: crystal, upgrades, the armory, "hunt harvesters", the warden rule, "game 11", "spend everything". No-op is `-` (tool description and schema changed with it).
7. `X` labels are named as enemies in the packet section; a label from `A` is the unit list, several may be joined.

## Bench and rules changed with the strategy

`push` now expects a hold (ore 80 with the queue at 2 buys nothing; the old predicate is exported as `pushOld` for rescoring older arms); `remembered` expects the seven out to come back to the post; `opening` accepts the barracks only; `train` accepts a turret or riflemen (Buy 4 puts the turret first at t46); `search`/`search2` accept the mirror as well as the field. Trajectory rules: `push8` → `push15`, plus `noSally` (under 15 with the rally home, no push beyond 30). `wave`, `core`, `capped`, `nobarracks`, `dry`, `repair`, `turret` unchanged.

## Bench result

Amended cases, 5 repeats: game32 68% → game38 + `--ashfall-guide` 92%; `remembered` 1/5 is the only miss. Three rounds got there: 78% on the text alone (the model skipped the turret line 4/5 on an absence check and never recalled the group out), 82% with `(no tu)` on `B` and `out` marks on `A`/`P` (turret 5/5), 92% after the heartbeat was taken off the `T` line under guide (the model answers `-` on any `T hb` packet whatever the prompt says: 7/7 on a bank of 135 with no turret) and the parser learned to take a label pasted with its state letter or `out`. Arm files in `runs/bench/`; the four reviewer reports are there too.

## Game 38 live (hub 58) and the revision

The first live game did the opening as written (barracks at 12 s, 6 then 12 harvesters, 19 riflemen and the turret by 4:30, no sally) and then lost the whole army in 8 s: the commit at 4:32 with 20 went to the mirror and met 21 dug-in riflemen, because the AI had not attacked yet and its entire army was home. The gate "nothing visible" was satisfied on a quiet packet; the wins had committed on the packet where a wave had just died. Revision: the commit needs `T kill` or `D E-` with `X` clear (a quiet packet never commits), a `dug` cluster as big as mine while the rally is out sends everyone back to the post, the hard turret line is gone at the user's direction (the turret is the agent's call, kept in Keep as advice), riflemen train while the queue is under 3, and the second barracks comes before workers past 6. Bench rules `turret` and `push15` were dropped with those lines. Bench after the revision: 83% on the full arm, then 21/25 on a partial rerun of the affected cases after Buy 1 took "supply within 2 of cap" and Buy 4 "fewer than 3 queued"; `train` (a `T none` packet at s8/10 with two riflemen queued) is the open miss at 1/5, `remembered` 1/5.

## Not done, worth knowing

The rules reference still carries the game's own prose (THREATS, REMEMBERED) that the packet renders differently; harmless. The `search` second step (nearest unexplored field once the mirror is seen empty) is untested live. `wholeBall` in the trajectory rules will flag a straggler `am` under Army 3 as a piece. The reserve A/B campaign was paused at 1 win in 4 and should restart on this prompt.
